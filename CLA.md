Contributions to this repository are accepted under the **OpenCTEM Individual
Contributor License Agreement, version 1.0**. Its full text, including the
party that receives the license, is published at
<https://docs.openctem.io/legal/cla/> (source: `legal/cla.md` in
[openctemio/docs](https://github.com/openctemio/docs)).

In short (this summary is not part of the agreement; the published text
governs):

- You keep the copyright in your contribution. The agreement grants a
  license; it does not transfer ownership.
- You grant a broad copyright and patent license, including the right to
  offer the project under other terms (for example a commercial license)
  next to its open source license.
- A contribution included in a release stays available, in that release,
  under the open source license this repository carried when you submitted
  it.
- You accept it once; it covers every repository of the `openctemio`
  organization, and your earlier contributions too.

**How to sign:** open your pull request. The `cla` check comments on it if a
commit author has not accepted the agreement yet. Read the agreement, then
post this comment on the pull request:

```text
I have read the CLA Document and I hereby sign the CLA
```

If you contribute as part of your job, your employer may own your work: see
[CLA-CORPORATE.md](CLA-CORPORATE.md). More detail, including what record is
kept, is in [CONTRIBUTING.md](CONTRIBUTING.md#contributor-license-agreement).
