### Security: complete reports from a trusted collector may refresh its own assets

- A collector report names no command, so it could create assets but never change one, and a re-collected inventory refreshed nothing. Now a report with `coverage_type: full` from a collector of the organization at trust level `trusted` (push ingest in effect) may change the existing assets its own collector source (sensor, tool and `metadata.source_ref`) created, and when the report is committed the assets that source created and no longer lists are marked not seen (status `stale`, a `disappeared` history row). Nothing is ever deleted (RFC-040 §12).
- Never changed or marked: an asset another source or a person created, an asset with a person's manual attribute lock or manual status override, anything of another organization. A partial or incremental report never marks anything; an empty report, a report that lost assets on the way, or one that would drop more than half (and more than 100) of the source's assets marks nothing; an older report sent again cannot overwrite newer values or mark assets a newer report listed. A finding a person resolved stays resolved.
- Migration 002081 adds `collector_assets` (tenant-scoped, composite foreign keys to the asset and the sensor). Assets collectors created before it are not owned by any collector until the collector creates them again.
