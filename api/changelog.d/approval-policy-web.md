### Added: the web console edits and explains the approval policy

- Settings → Finding approvals has an Approval policy section: the tier per request kind and risk class (only tiers at or above the platform minimum are offered, each cell shows its minimum and default), the security pool group and its strict mode, excluding the assignee, re-authentication for every approval, acceptance caps per class, the decision deadline, the EPSS threshold and what a material change does. A waiting relaxation shows when it takes effect and can be canceled. Read-only without `settings:write`. RFC-111.
- The request dialog for a false positive or an accepted risk says which approval it needs and why (the reasons), how many people may approve and from which pool, whether they re-authenticate, the longest acceptance (the date picker stops there) and the shortest note, and warns when the organization lacks eligible approvers.
- The approvals inbox shows each request's tier and the sign-off progress of a two-approver request, says how many approvals are still needed after yours, and explains a refusal (asset owner, assignee, not in the security pool, already approved, nobody eligible) in the reader's language.
- "Cancel the change" on a waiting approval-settings change now sends the explicit `cancel_pending` flag instead of saving the current values again.
