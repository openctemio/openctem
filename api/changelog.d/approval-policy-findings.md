### Behaviour change: finding approvals follow the approval policy (tiers by risk, two approvers, step-up, asset owners excluded)

- Accepting the risk of a high finding (severity high, P1, a validated exploit, EPSS at or above 0.5, or a medium finding on a crown-jewel or internet-exposed asset) needs an approver from the security pool who re-authenticated in the last 10 minutes; a critical finding (severity critical, P0, KEV) needs two such approvers by default. Low and medium acceptances still need one other person. RFC-111.
- The owners of a finding's asset (user owners and members of owner groups) can no longer approve its false positive or accepted risk, and never decide a false positive alone.
- `POST /api/v1/approvals/{id}/approve` can answer 200 with the request still `pending` (`approvals_collected` < `approvals_required`), 403 `APPROVER_NOT_ELIGIBLE` with `details.reason`, 409 `NO_ELIGIBLE_APPROVER`, or 403 `STEP_UP_REQUIRED`. Each refusal is audited `finding_approval.approval_refused`; each partial approval `finding_approval.signed`.
- `settings.finding_approvals.policy` (PUT `/api/v1/organization/settings/finding-approvals/`): tier per kind and class, security pool group, exclude assignee, step-up for every tier, class caps, decision deadline, EPSS threshold. It can only add constraints above the platform floors; a relaxing change takes effect 24 hours later. An organization with only two people that needs to accept critical risks can set `accepted.critical` to `security`.
- `GET /api/v1/findings/{id}/approvals/policy?status=accepted` explains how a request would be decided (tier, reasons, eligible approvers, cap).
- Migrations `002796_approval_policy_signoffs` (+ `002797` validate) and `002798_finding_activity_approval_signed` (+ `002799` validate): nullable columns and NOT VALID checks, then a separate validation; no long locks.
