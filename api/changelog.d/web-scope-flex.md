### Added: flexible scope in the web console

- Scope page: "Add a list" pastes names, wildcards, addresses, ranges and URLs into scope in one request, with every line's outcome; a banner offers the addresses your in-scope names resolve to (shared CDN and hosting addresses left out) for one click into scope.
- Settings › Scope: the switch "Confirming an asset adds it to scope" (on by default). Confirming assets in the review queue or on an asset adds their exact entries; when a re-authentication is needed the console asks for it and adds them.
- Tenable.sc connector: "Put this connector's inventory in scope" (on by default).
- A reusable "Add N to scope" button for the targets a scan review refused (origin `refusal_fix`). RFC-054 §14.
