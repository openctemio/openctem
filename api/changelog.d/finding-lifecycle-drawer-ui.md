### Added: findings show their sub-status, and the status menu offers the lifecycle's moves

- The findings list, the drawer and the finding page show the organization's sub-status next to the
  status.
- The status menu lists the moves the organization's lifecycle allows, including moves onto a
  sub-status, with what each needs; a move that needs a longer note or fields (ticket link, fix reference,
  change ID, target version, due date, compensating control) asks for them before it is sent.
- A bulk status change reports up front the findings whose move needs fields or a longer note.
