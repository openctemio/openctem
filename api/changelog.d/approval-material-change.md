### Added: standing accepted risks and one-person false positives react to material changes

- A new controller (`approval-material-change`, every 10 minutes) checks approved accepted risks and false positives one person decided against the facts they were decided on. An accepted risk ends (the finding is open again with a new SLA clock) when the finding now needs a higher approval tier (for example KEV added, severity or priority up, EPSS reaching the threshold, a validated exploit, the asset newly internet exposed or a crown jewel) or its cap now ends sooner; otherwise it is flagged for review. A one-person false positive is flagged, or withdrawn when the organization chose `on_material_change.single_person_fp: revoke`; `on_material_change.accepted: end_on_any_change` ends acceptances on any change. RFC-111 §6.
- Each action is on the finding's timeline, in the audit log (`finding_approval.material_change_ended|flagged|revoked`) and notified in-app to the requester and approvers.
- Migrations `002786_approval_material_change` (+ `002787` validate): `facts_baseline` column, a partial index on standing decisions, review reason `material_change_accepted` (the CHECK also keeps `acceptance_exceeds_cap`, which an earlier rewrite of the constraint had dropped).
