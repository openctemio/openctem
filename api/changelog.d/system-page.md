### Added: System page in the platform console (read-only)
- `GET /api/v1/admin/system` and Console > System status: service health (api, postgres, redis, web, gateway, connected sensors), free space of the configured data volumes and the database size, the last successful backup, sensor CA and TLS certificate expiry, the running version and the version-check result. Readable by every platform administrator role including the read-only one; aggregates only, no tenant data, secrets or paths.
- A system watcher sets `openctem_system_*` metrics and emails the platform administrators when free space falls below 20% (warning) or 10% (critical), the last backup is older than 26 hours, or a certificate expires within 30 days (warning) or 7 days (critical). Configure with `SYSTEM_STORAGE_PATHS`, `SYSTEM_BACKUP_STATUS_FILE`, `SYSTEM_TLS_CERT_FILES`, `SYSTEM_WEB_HEALTH_URL`, `SYSTEM_GATEWAY_HEALTH_URL`, `SYSTEM_WATCH_INTERVAL`.
