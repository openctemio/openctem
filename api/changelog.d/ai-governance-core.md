### Added: AI results are governed suggestions with provenance

- Every AI result is stored as a typed suggestion of an AI run (`ai_runs`, `ai_suggestions`) with its provenance: executor, provider and model, prompt template id and digest, output schema, bundle digest, fields sent and withheld, tokens and cost. The direction of a suggestion (adds information, raises risk, lowers risk) is computed by the platform from the finding's facts, never taken from the model.
- Per organization and use case (triage, remediation, summary): mode off, suggest or auto-apply (`tenant_ai_use_cases`, `GET/PUT /api/v1/organization/settings/ai/use-cases`). Auto-apply applies additive suggestions only; risk-lowering suggestions always become approval requests, and severity or priority changes always wait for a person. Turning auto-apply on, or raising the data class ceiling above internal, needs a recent sign-in and is a critical audit row.
- A person applies, dismisses or undoes a suggestion (`/api/v1/ai/suggestions/{id}/apply|dismiss|undo`, `/api/v1/ai/runs/{id}/undo`) through the same paths and permissions as the change made by hand; undo refuses when the field changed since. When people override 20 % of the last 50 decisions, auto-apply falls back to suggest until an administrator turns it on again.
- Migration `003492_ai_governance_core` creates the three tables and copies `ai_triage_results` into them (a run keeps the triage result's id); `ai_triage_results` stays until a later contract step. It also allows the finding timeline entries `ai_suggestion_applied` and `ai_suggestion_undone`. No operator action.
