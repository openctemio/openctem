### Changed: New Scan options, and one target picker for Quick scan and Scan these assets

- New Scan Options shows where the scan runs (Auto, your sensors, platform where offered) and the scan profile; job size, timeout and retries are under Advanced. The sensor preference moved here from the first step.
- Quick scan and Scan these assets use the New Scan target parts: per-line format, repeats counted once, the live scope check and removable chips. Pasted targets may be separated by newlines, commas, semicolons or spaces.
