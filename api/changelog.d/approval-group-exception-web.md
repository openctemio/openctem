### Added: the web console asks for and decides an exception on a remediation task

- The remediation task page has a Request exception action (with `findings:remediation:write` and `findings:write`): one accepted risk or false positive over the task's open findings, with a reason code, a note of at least the policy's length and, for an accepted risk, an end date. A live preview shows the group's approval tier and why, who may approve, and each finding with its tier and end, marking the ones shortened to their own limit and the ones left out with the reason. A pending exception shows on the task (tier, approvals so far, member count, deadline) with a link to the inbox. RFC-111 §7.
- The approvals inbox has a Group exceptions view: the task, status, tier and approvals, member count and deadline per exception, and a side panel with its members and Approve, Reject (a reason is required) and Cancel (the requester only), with the same handling as a single approval (approvals still needed, refusal reasons, re-authentication, already decided). A member request in the findings view links to its group instead of offering its own Approve or Reject.
