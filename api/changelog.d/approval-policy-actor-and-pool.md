### Security: approval requests made by an actor need one more sign-off; the security pool is harder to stack

- A request a linked ticket, VEX or AI triage makes (no person behind it) needs one sign-off more than its tier asks, so the person who moved the ticket cannot complete it with one account. A ticket's proposed acceptance end is within the organization's cap for the finding.
- A member of the security pool group counts as the pool only 24 hours after joining it, and adding or removing a member of that group (or deleting it) needs a recent re-authentication.
