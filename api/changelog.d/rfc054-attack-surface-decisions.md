### Changed: the scope model records the attack surface review decisions

- RFC-054 §14.5 records the decisions now in force:
  - a scope entry lets scans reach a pending name;
  - children follow their host;
  - a person's confirmation adds the exact entry;
  - addresses behind in-scope names become one suggestion;
  - the Suggestions tab replaces the review queue;
  - connector assets are in scope by default.
- It also gives the threat model of each decision. R3 and R4 are adopted, and
  the connector default changes from off to on.
