### Changed: AI assistants (MCP) settings are organized in tabs, with a request-and-approve flow

- Settings, AI access (MCP) is now **AI assistants (MCP)** with a status line and tabs: Connect (everyone), Applications, Connections and Policy.
- Applications lists members' access requests (Approve / Decline), the **Approved publishers** (a validated list with Add publisher and Remove, replacing the free-text "Trusted application hosts" box; platform-trusted hosts are shown read only), the applications registered by the organization, and, folded under **Advanced**, "Allow unverified applications", which now asks for confirmation before it turns on.
- Policy groups the settings into Access, **What assistants may do** (read scopes, and the comment-adding scope, off by default, with each comment confirmed by the member) and Limits.
- The consent page of a blocked application offers **Request access** to members and **Allow <publisher> for <organization>** to those who may change the policy.
- All AI assistant pages (settings, connected applications, consent and change confirmation) are translated (English and Vietnamese). Alert titles now wrap instead of being cut off.
