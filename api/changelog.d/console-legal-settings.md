### Behaviour change: legal links, legal pages and security.txt are set in the admin console

- Console > System > Legal sets the terms and privacy links of the sign-in and sign-up pages, whether the built-in legal pages are served and their values, and the `/.well-known/security.txt` contact and policy (`GET`/`PUT /api/v1/admin/settings/legal`, super admin with a fresh authenticator code, audited). The web console reads them from the public `GET /api/v1/legal`; changes show within a minute, without a redeploy.
- **Removed:** the web console no longer reads `LEGAL_TERMS_URL`, `LEGAL_PRIVACY_URL`, `LEGAL_PAGES_ENABLED`, `LEGAL_ORGANIZATION_NAME`, `LEGAL_SERVICE_NAME`, `LEGAL_CONTACT_EMAIL`, `LEGAL_WEBSITE_URL`, `LEGAL_DOCS_URL`, `LEGAL_ADDRESS`, `LEGAL_JURISDICTION`, `LEGAL_EFFECTIVE_DATE`, `SECURITY_CONTACT`, `SECURITY_POLICY_URL`, `SECURITY_TXT_ENABLED`, `NEXT_PUBLIC_TERMS_URL`, `NEXT_PUBLIC_PRIVACY_URL`. **Upgrade note:** enter their values in the console after upgrading; until then the defaults apply (OpenCTEM contacts, no links, legal pages off).
