### Added: Transfer ownership on the Members page

- The owner picks "Transfer ownership..." on a member's row. A dialog shows whether the member qualifies (active, two-factor on, not managed by another organization), lets the owner choose what they become, and asks for confirmation when an outside member would become internal.
- While a transfer is open, the owner sees who was asked and can cancel it. The member asked sees what changes and can accept (after signing in again) or decline. English and Vietnamese.
