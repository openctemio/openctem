### Changed: Scope page with In scope, Out of scope and Approvals (web)

- Scoping › Scope lists what scans may touch on three tabs (research/53
  W1/W3): In scope (entries), Out of scope (exclusions, which always win) and
  Approvals, one list of every entry and exclusion waiting for a second
  person, with who asked, why, what it covers, for how long and the approvals
  so far. Several changes can be approved at once; the requester never can.
- "Put out of scope" adds an exclusion with a detected kind, a required
  reason and an optional end; it waits for another approver.
- Widening row actions say so ("Activate (may need approval)", "Extend by 7
  days (may need approval)", "Lift (scans may reach it)"); there are no
  inline toggles.
- An empty In scope tab shows the three steps to get started (add your
  domain, prove it, discovery finds the rest), worded for the operator's
  active-proof mode.
