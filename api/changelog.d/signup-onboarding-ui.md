### Added: onboarding, domain settings and join requests in the web console

- A signed-in account without an organization sees its invitations, the organization of its
  verified email domain (Join or Ask to join, then "waiting for approval") and the create form when
  allowed. Joining opens the organization at once.
- Settings > Domains (owners change, admins read): who may join with each verified single sign-on
  domain, discoverability, requiring SSO and blocking new organizations for accounts on it.
- Settings > Members shows waiting requests to join, approved as viewer or member or rejected.
- Sign-up refused because the domain requires its organization's SSO sends the person to sign in
  there. All text in English and Vietnamese.
