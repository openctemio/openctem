### Changed: platform console navigation and overview

- The console sidebar groups pages as Customers, Scanning, Security, Settings
  and Monitoring; every scanning page sits under Scanning, and the personal
  security keys moved to the sidebar footer next to the signed-in
  administrator.
- A super admin acting read-only now sees every page; a page that needs a
  higher acting role offers the elevation in place instead of saying "Super
  admin only".
- The Overview's "Needs attention" list now shows services that do not
  answer, volumes running out of space, failed, stale or unreported backups,
  certificates close to expiry and newer releases.
- Lists that are empty without any search or filter no longer suggest
  adjusting one. The console has a "Skip to content" link, and the temporary
  password form and shell messages are translated.
