### Added: Settings > AI > AI analysts, one AI badge with accountable owners, and AI data masking

- Settings > AI has an AI analysts section. An AI analyst takes part in triage like a person, as its own service account. The list shows each analyst's name, preset, status (active, paused or disabled, with the reason: no active owner, attestation overdue, paused by an administrator, missing permissions), accountable owners and the date its attestation is due. People who hold `service_accounts:manage` can create an analyst (name, description, preset Suggest or Triage with a one-line explanation of each, and at least one accountable person or team), change its owners or preset, resume, disable and delete it, each after a confirmation; creating, changing owners or preset, resuming and deleting ask for a recent sign-in. An accountable person can also attest to and pause their own analyst. Everyone else sees the list without actions.
- Every AI-made item carries one shared AI badge. An AI analyst shows as "AI analyst {name}" with a muted second line "Accountable: {owners}"; an AI application connected over MCP and the built-in AI triage result keep their labels. In a finding's activity feed, a change an AI analyst made names the analyst and its accountable owners from the activity's provenance; older AI rows without provenance read "AI assistant". A gate test fails when a component that renders an AI actor does not use the badge.
- Settings > AI has a Data masking section: switches per class of value masked in internal data (hosts, IP addresses, e-mail addresses, user names, URL ids and parameters, organization names, ticket ids), the subdomain label switch and extra terms (up to 200, 3 to 128 characters). Secrets are shown as always masked and locked, restricted data as always fully masked. A preview shows, for one finding id, the masked text the model would get (plain text), the placeholders with their class only, and the fields sent and withheld, with a note on what masking does not hide. Saving needs a recent sign-in; a change that masks less asks first.
- The console shows nothing of the AI analysts or masking sections until the API offers their routes.
