# Corporate Contributor License Agreement

If your employer has rights in the work you contribute, your employer accepts
the **OpenCTEM Corporate Contributor License Agreement, version 1.0** before
your contribution can be merged. Its full text, the signing form and the list
of designated employees (Schedule A) are published at
<https://docs.openctem.io/legal/cla-corporate/> (source:
`legal/cla-corporate.md` in [openctemio/docs](https://github.com/openctemio/docs)).

1. A person authorized to sign for the company completes and signs the form
   and Schedule A, and e-mails it to **info@openctem.io** with the subject
   "OpenCTEM Corporate CLA".
2. Each designated employee also posts the sign comment on their first pull
   request (see [CLA.md](CLA.md)), so the check can link their GitHub account.
3. To change the list of designated employees, the company's CLA manager
   e-mails an updated Schedule A.

The company keeps the copyright in its contributions; the agreement grants a
license, it does not transfer ownership.
