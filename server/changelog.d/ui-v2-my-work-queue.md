### Changed: My Work follows the list pattern on a phone and on a desktop (redesign branch)

- The page title carries the one main action (Triage, with how many findings wait); Refresh moved into the
  list's title row, which names the open tab and shows its count once.
- The sentence that says what a tab holds is one line from tablet width up and sits behind an info button on a
  phone, so it no longer pushes the list down.
- Finding queues (Assigned to me, My team, Acceptances expiring): the locked rule, the "SLA due in 72 hours"
  toggle and "Top N of M" sit on the line under the toolbar; the toolbar's end has Triage these (the main
  action) and View all as a named icon button. Nothing in that row is cut or overlapped at 390 px.
- On a phone each finding is a card: the title on the full width (two lines at most), priority and severity on
  one line, where the finding is, then the deadline and the status on one line; no mark wraps inside itself.
- No behaviour, permission, count or key changes: the tab counts still equal the list totals, the review keys
  (j, k, Enter, a, r), Approve with its sign-in step, Remind and Withdraw are unchanged.
- Docs: none (internal redesign branch; no user guide covers this layout).
