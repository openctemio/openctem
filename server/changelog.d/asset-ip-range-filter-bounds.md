### Security: address range filters are bounded; asset child reads pinned to the tenant

- An address range filter (assets `ip` / `in_cidr`, findings `asset_ip`, query language `asset.ip`, saved views, smart group rules `filter.in_cidr`) is at most a /8 of IPv4 or a /32 of IPv6. `0.0.0.0/0`, `::/0` and other wider ranges are refused with 400 and the bound named. A smart group rule stored with such a range keeps evaluating; saving or previewing it again needs a narrower range. A saved view holding one is refused when applied until it is edited.
- One filter binds at most 50 addresses or ranges over all its address conditions (each is one index range scan in the query). A filter document with more is refused with 400 naming the limit, instead of running hundreds of range scans.
- Defence in depth: three asset reads of child tables keyed by asset id are now also pinned to the asset's tenant: the "rescore pending" mark (`asset_risk_queue`), the "no attribution record" filter (`asset_attributions`) and the open findings counted by asset statistics (`findings`). Plans measured: the first two keep their primary-key probe; the statistics read now uses the tenant index instead of scanning every organization's findings.
