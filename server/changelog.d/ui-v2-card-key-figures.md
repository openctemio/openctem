### Changed: a phone card shows the list's key figures first

- The automatic phone card of a list has room for two fields. Columns that hold the list's key figures (a count, a risk score, a severity, a priority, a due date) now take those places before descriptive columns, so the asset groups card shows Assets and Findings (also "Findings 0") instead of Criticality and Assets. The fields are still shown in column order (every list without its own phone card).
- Docs: web/docs/ui-style-contract.md (automatic phone card)
